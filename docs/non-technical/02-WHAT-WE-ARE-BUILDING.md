# What Atexgrove is building

## The long-term idea

Atexgrove aims to become a trusted environment for land development. Over time it may connect land information, verification, development planning, professional services, projects, construction activities, documents, and property operations.

That whole vision is not the first prototype.

## The first useful service

The accepted first direction is a controlled land-verification and development-feasibility pilot for Abuja/FCT. This is a limited pilot service, not a government registry, legal certification service or public land marketplace.

A customer should be able to:

1. Start a case themselves or receive authorized staff assistance to start one.
2. Move through request received, documents in, document checks, site and map, development options, and report.
3. Upload supporting evidence privately when the applicable document-readiness rules permit it.
4. Respond to requests for clarification or better evidence.
5. See the source, review state and authority level of important information.
6. Receive a limited reviewed report that states what is known, uncertain and outside Atexgrove's authority.

Authorized staff should be able to:

1. Create an assisted case with the customer's consent and a recorded audit trail.
2. See submitted cases in a work queue.
3. Have a human lead assign or hand over a case, with automation limited to recommendations.
4. Accept work when capacity permits or decline with a recorded reason.
5. Review evidence and record where information came from.
6. Record unresolved questions and limitations.
7. Enter transparent feasibility assumptions.
8. Prepare a report for a second, discipline-appropriate review before consequential conclusions are delivered.

## What the report must and must not mean

The initial report is limited to document intake/completeness, cross-document consistency, provenance, available professional review and separately labelled official-source evidence. It can also show transparent development assumptions and estimates where the required inputs exist. It must clearly distinguish all of these from confirmation by a government authority or another legally recognized body.

Atexgrove must never imply that an internal review automatically proves ownership, title validity, planning approval, survey accuracy, or legal compliance.

## Who the prototype serves

The pilot user types are expected to include:

- customers requesting verification or feasibility work;
- internal operations staff;
- reviewers or qualified professionals;
- organization owners and administrators with limited responsibilities.

No staff team exists yet. The founder/CTO may act as pilot lead/operator, but a genuinely separate qualified specialist must perform the second review for conclusions in their discipline. Named pilot users, contracted reviewers and availability remain open.

## What comes later

After the report workflow is proven, later phases may add:

- controlled land discovery and maps;
- project workspaces;
- professional involvement;
- broader construction workflows;
- property management;
- payments;
- mobile and offline field work;
- carefully governed AI assistance.

These future areas must not be shown as working features before they are genuinely connected and tested.

The pilot collects no fees. A screen may demonstrate an estimated fee or approval step only when it clearly says that no payment is collected. AI must not process real pilot documents; any early AI demonstration must use synthetic evidence and remain advisory.

## Interface approach

The prototype will use a simple working interface. It does not need polished marketing visuals before the main journey works. Each important screen must still show clear loading, empty, error, permission-denied, and success states.
