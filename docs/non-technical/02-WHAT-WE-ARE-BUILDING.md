# What Atexgrove is building

## The long-term idea

Atexgrove aims to become a trusted environment for land development. Over time it may connect land information, verification, development planning, professional services, projects, construction activities, documents, and property operations.

That whole vision is not the first prototype.

## The first useful service

The proposed first commercial service is land verification and development feasibility in Abuja.

A customer should be able to:

1. Open a verification case for a site.
2. Describe the site and the help required.
3. Upload supporting documents privately.
4. Submit the case for review.
5. Respond if staff request more information.
6. Receive a reviewed Land & Development Report.

Authorized staff should be able to:

1. See submitted cases in a work queue.
2. Assign or hand over a case.
3. Review evidence and record where information came from.
4. Record unresolved questions and limitations.
5. Enter transparent feasibility assumptions.
6. Prepare, review, approve, and deliver a report.

## What the report must and must not mean

The report can explain supplied documents, professional observations, planning assumptions, development estimates, and unresolved matters. It must clearly distinguish these from confirmation by a government authority or another legally recognized body.

Atexgrove must never imply that an internal review automatically proves ownership, title validity, planning approval, survey accuracy, or legal compliance.

## Who the prototype serves

The first users are expected to include:

- customers requesting verification or feasibility work;
- internal operations staff;
- reviewers or qualified professionals;
- organization owners and administrators with limited responsibilities.

The exact launch roles still need founder confirmation.

## What comes later

After the report workflow is proven, later phases may add:

- controlled land discovery and maps;
- project workspaces;
- professional involvement;
- broader construction workflows;
- property management;
- payments;
- mobile and offline field work;
- carefully governed AI assistance.

These future areas must not be shown as working features before they are genuinely connected and tested.

## Interface approach

The prototype will use a simple working interface. It does not need polished marketing visuals before the main journey works. Each important screen must still show clear loading, empty, error, permission-denied, and success states.
