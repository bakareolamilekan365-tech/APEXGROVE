# Decisions still needed

The project can begin Phase 2 with sensible demonstration assumptions, but the following decisions are needed before handling real customers or real evidence.

## Most urgent

1. Is the first release only a demonstration, or will real customers use it?
2. Is the Land & Development Report definitely the first paid or commercial deliverable?
3. Who prepares, reviews, approves, and signs that report?
4. Which staff responsibilities exist at launch, and which people may hold more than one responsibility?
5. Which title or land checks will Atexgrove actually perform, and which will it merely record as supplied?
6. Will real customer documents be stored? If yes, for how long and who may access them?

## Product direction to confirm

The current roadmap assumes:

- Abuja first;
- verification and feasibility before public land discovery;
- no payments in the first prototype;
- maps support the case but do not prove legal boundaries;
- AI remains advisory and cannot approve or authenticate evidence.

If this direction is final, the verification-first roadmap decision should be marked **Accepted** instead of **Proposed**.

## Trust and legal decisions

- Who approves the wording used for trust labels and limitations?
- What legal terms and privacy notice are required before real documents are stored?
- Who is responsible if data is incorrect, unavailable, or disclosed improperly?
- Which professional or authority can confirm each type of evidence?

## Ownership decisions

The company should eventually own the repository, Supabase organization, domains, and other production accounts. Personal accounts may be acceptable during early development but should not become an undocumented long-term dependency.
