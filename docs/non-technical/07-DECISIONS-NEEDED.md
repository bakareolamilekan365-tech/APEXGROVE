# Pilot decisions and remaining questions

The verification-first Abuja/FCT pilot direction was approved on 2026-10-14. The project can design and test the workflow with synthetic/redacted evidence, but it cannot accept real client evidence until the remaining people, policy and operational questions are closed.

## Most urgent

1. Who are the first pilot participants, and when will the first real case begin?
2. Which named qualified specialists will perform the second review and sign conclusions in their discipline?
3. What is the final report name/template, and which conclusions may it contain?
4. What are the exact retention period, deletion exceptions and backup treatment for real evidence?
5. What are the company registration details, accountable privacy/support contacts and approved legal text?
6. Which survey, zoning and land-use datasets may be used, under what permission/licence?

## Approved product direction

The current roadmap now records:

- an Abuja/FCT controlled pilot first;
- a six-stage verification-first case journey before public land discovery;
- both customer-created and staff-assisted cases;
- no payments in the pilot (a clearly labelled demonstration estimate is permitted);
- maps support the case but do not prove legal boundaries;
- AI remains advisory, cannot approve/authenticate evidence, and will not process real pilot documents;
- Atexgrove operational review, professional review and official confirmation remain separate; and
- synthetic/redacted evidence is used until the real-document readiness gate is complete.

The report is deliberately limited to document intake/consistency, available professional review and separately labelled official-source evidence. Unsupported questions remain inconclusive rather than being presented as verified.

## Trust and legal decisions

- Which named legal, survey and planning reviewers approve the wording and discipline-specific conclusions?
- Finalize and professionally review the Privacy Notice, Terms of Service, pilot/document consent, cookie treatment, retention/deletion rules, complaints process and authority-boundary disclaimers.
- Who is responsible if data is incorrect, unavailable, or disclosed improperly?
- Confirm the exact official sources and professional remit for each evidence type.

## Ownership decisions

"Account and infrastructure ownership" means deciding which legal entity/account controls GitHub, Supabase, hosting, domain/DNS, operational email providers, billing and recovery access. The company should eventually own or formally control these production resources, with named recovery administrators and documented handover. Personal accounts may be acceptable during early development but must not become an undocumented single-person dependency.
