## 1. Information-source labels (from blueprint s.4)
Every fact shown about a land case should carry one of:
1. Seller-reported
2. Document received, not independently authenticated
3. Checked by an authorised professional
4. Confirmed through an authoritative source
5. AI-generated estimate or suggestion
6. Unresolved

Each label needs, where relevant: source, date, reviewer, scope, and limitations.
Rule: an unverified document or AI output is never presented as official confirmation of ownership, title or approval.
